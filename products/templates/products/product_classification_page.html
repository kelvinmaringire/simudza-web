{# 4. How classification works #}

How does Simudza classify a product?

Classification follows evidence about materials, manufacturing, finishing, and documented economic links — not marketing claims alone.

  1. 1

    What is the product?

    Identify the finished good as listed.

  2. 2

    Where are its primary materials produced?

    Agricultural or plant-based origin points to Grown.

  3. 3

    Where does manufacturing or processing happen?

    Substantial local manufacture points to Made.

  4. 4

    Where is it packaged or assembled?

    Local finishing may add Packaged or Assembled.

  5. 5

    Is there another documented Zimbabwean economic connection?

    Ownership or business links may support Supports Zimbabwe.

  6. 6

    Apply every classification that honestly fits

    Use all that evidence supports — skip what it does not.
